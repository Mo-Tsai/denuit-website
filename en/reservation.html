Before you book

Reservations open at 12:00 noon on the 1st of each month, for the current and following month — lunch and dinner alike.

Reservations are accepted exclusively through inline below — no phone, DM or third-party bookings.

Up to six guests per booking. For more than six, or to reserve the whole restaurant, write to info@denuit.com.tw two months ahead.

A credit card guarantees each booking. Nothing is charged. American Express cannot be used as a guarantee.

We send a confirmation seven days before your visit.

To change or cancel, please let us know three days ahead. Later cancellations and no-shows carry an ingredient fee: NT$3,800 per guest at lunch, NT$4,800 at dinner.

Dining notes

Guests aged 12 and above. Each guest takes a full menu.

Please allow about three hours. Service begins once the whole party has arrived. Tables are held for 10 minutes.

Smart casual. Gentlemen, please avoid shorts, sleeveless tops and slippers. Ladies, please avoid slippers and heeled slides. Please avoid strong fragrance.

We are unable to prepare vegetarian, gluten-free or dairy-free menus. Please note acute allergies or religious restrictions when booking. We will do our best.

The private room seats up to six. Minimum spend is NT$30,000 at lunch and NT$42,000 at dinner, plus 10%.

For a birthday or anniversary, we can write a message on the plate. A mini cake is NT$450, ordered three days ahead. A cake of your own carries a NT$500 cleaning fee.
