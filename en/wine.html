The full list

I

Domaine Rolet Crémant Blanc de Blancs “Cœur de Chardonnay” 2020

Jura, France · 100% Chardonnay

Lemon peel, mineral and a faint salinity; bright acidity and fine bubbles, with notes of yeast and age. With the first three small bites, its salinity and acidity lift the natural sweetness of the seafood and vegetables.

II

Mewstone Riesling 2023

Tasmania, Australia

Lime, white flowers and a high-acid line; clean and fine. Its bright acidity and floral notes sharpen the shellfish and the herbs in the dish.

III

Amelie Guillot Arbois Floral Blanc “Vieilles Vignes” 2023

Jura, France · 80% Savagnin, 20% Chardonnay

Savoury, with almond and mineral notes; concentrated and layered. Jura answers the vin jaune in the dish, giving the sauce and the fish more depth.

IV

Domaine Duseigneur “L’Astrolabe” 2023

Lirac, Rhône, France · 100% Syrah

Red berries, violet and spice; open and warm. Its blackberry and black-pepper notes add smoke and spice to the meat course.

V

Domaine Arlaud Morey-Saint-Denis 2020

Burgundy, France · 100% Pinot Noir

Wood, flowers and dark berries; fine-bodied, with a forest-floor and herbal finish. Ripe red fruit and soft tannins — the classic Burgundy match for duck — balance its richness and echo the smoke, wood and spice of the dish.

VI

Ximénez-Spínola Old Harvest

Jerez, Spain · 100% Pedro Ximénez

Chocolate, hazelnut and oxidative age; rich and full, with a caramel, aged finish. It reads as an extension of the main dessert, echoing the black garlic and the pineapple’s acidity — best taken together with the dessert.
