Signatures

Signatures

Some dishes stay, and become signatures. Some belong to a single season, and become a chapter.

I

Quail / Morel / Vin jaune

Quail from Xikou, Chiayi: the leg wrapped with foie gras and braised in vin jaune, the breast filled with morel and chicken-liver mousse. A Kei Koo classic.

II

King salmon / Passion fruit / Gooseberry

Glacial king salmon from Mount Cook, cured in citrus, with Taiwanese passion fruit and gooseberry juice and nasturtium oil.

III

Abalone / Foie gras / Dashi

A kombu and bonito broth between abalone and foie gras; green peppercorn, pickled daikon, a few drops of sherry vinegar.

IV

Pineapple / Black garlic / Cacao

Black garlic, as dessert. The brightness of Taiwanese pineapple, a cacao mousse, and a faint, grown-up bitterness.

V

Lamb / Harissa / Pepper leaf

New Zealand highland lamb rack with harissa, wrapped in native Taiwanese pepper leaf and seared.

VI

Lamb saddle / Longan wood / Rose wine

Inspired by Cantonese char siu: longan-wood smoke, and a lamb jus cooked with rose wine.

VII

Rose duck / Mango / Goji

Charcoal-grilled rose duck with two duck-bone sauces, and three Taiwanese mangoes: Irwin, Jinhuang and Black Fragrant.

VIII

Oyster / Plankton / Fennel

French oyster turned into ice cream, with fennel purée and plankton butter — revisited and refined from one season to the next.

IX

Golden eye snapper / Vin jaune / Zucchini flower

Charcoal-grilled golden eye snapper with a vin jaune butter mousse and a Breton curry foam — one rich, one light.

X

White asparagus / Chicken / Miso

One ingredient, two temperatures: a cold asparagus custard under a clear chicken jelly, and a hot velouté in a crisp shell.
